Privacy Policy

This policy describes the intended data handling of HR ONE SHOT AUTO (the “Application”), a desktop tool for AI-assisted video production and optional social media account integrations.

1. Information processed

Depending on features you choose to use, the Application may process product details, images, videos, scripts, generated audio, publishing schedules, application settings, and information returned by connected platforms, such as account identifiers, granted permissions, and authorization tokens.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to provide requested video creation, project management, account connection, and authorized publishing features; to show connection status; and to diagnose errors.

3. Storage and security

The Application is designed to use local storage for project data and settings. The exact location and protection of tokens depend on the installed version and its configuration. Do not assume all data is encrypted. The operator should protect the computer and keep API credentials private.

4. Third-party services

When enabled by the operator, the Application may transmit information necessary for requested functions to services such as TikTok, Meta/Facebook, and configured AI providers. Those services process data under their own policies. No third-party transfer occurs merely by visiting this informational website.

5. Data retention and deletion

Project files and local records may remain on the operator’s device until removed. Disconnecting an account may stop future access but may not delete previously generated files or information already sent to a platform. For data deletion or access questions, contact the operator using the address below. The operator should verify the actual deletion process before publishing this policy.

6. Children and eligibility

The Application should be used only where the user meets the applicable age and account eligibility requirements of connected platforms and services.

7. Policy changes

This policy may change as the Application and its integrations evolve.
